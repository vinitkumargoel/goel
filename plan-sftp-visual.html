Copy engine

The relay pipe

SFTP has no server-side copy, and libssh2 1.11 cannot send OpenSSH's copy-data@openssh.com. So bytes come off one session and go onto another through a bounded ring buffer, with backpressure both ways.

◀ source session · gsb_download gsb_upload · dest session ▶

Upload starts immediately rather than after a full staging download, so wall-clock is roughly halved and no temp disk is needed — a 200 GB move needs no 200 GB of free space.

Move, in order of preference

  1. Same server, same filesystem — one rename. Instant, atomic, no data moves. Exists today.
  2. Same server, different filesystem — rename fails with SSH_FX_FAILURE; fall back to relay, stat-verify, delete source. Fails outright today.
  3. Different servers — relay, stat-verify, delete source. No alternative without agent forwarding.
Partial failure is not rolled back. A folder move that dies at file 300 of 500 leaves both sides intact and reports exactly what moved. Automatic rollback of a half-finished move is how a failed operation becomes a data-loss event.
