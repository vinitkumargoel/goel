Commercial licensing

Free for people. Licensed for companies.

Goel° is source-available and free forever for personal use — and free for charities, schools and public bodies too, because the licence grants that outright. A paid licence is for commercial use: a business, a contractor billing clients, a product or service you sell, a corporate fleet. 01 · Eligibility

Do I need a licence?

The test is the nature of the use, not the type of organisation. If the use is not directed toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation, the licence already grants it — free, no permission needed. That is why charities, schools and public bodies are on the free side. A paid licence is for use that serves a business.

  • Hobby projects, home labs and home servers
  • Students and personal academic work (your own coursework, thesis or research)
  • Personal media, archives, backups and self-hosting
  • Charities and non-profits — including paid staff and IT-managed machines
  • Schools, colleges, universities and public research organisations, as institutions
  • Government institutions, and public safety, health or environmental protection bodies

  • Contractors and freelancers using it in billable client work, whoever the client is
  • Bundling, reselling, hosting or offering Goel° as part of a paid product or service
  • Company machines managed by MDM, Intune, Jamf, Munki, Ansible or a corporate fleet-imaging pipeline
  • Servers, CI runners, build agents and shared infrastructure at a business
  • Internal tooling built on GoelCore or the remote JSON API at a business
  • The commercially directed work of a charity, school or public body — a spin-out, or a service it sells (the rest of that organisation stays free)

The one-line rule: Goel° is released under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. It permits any purpose that is not intended for or directed toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation, and its Noncommercial Organizations clause permits charitable, educational, public research, public safety, health, environmental protection and government institutions outright — regardless of how they are funded. We do not charge for anything that licence already gives you. A commercial licence covers the uses that fall outside it, and replaces the public terms with ones your legal team can sign. Genuinely unsure which side you fall on? Ask — the honest answer is free and fast.

What we will never do: there is no trial clock, no licence key to enter, no activation call home and no locked features. The build a paying enterprise runs is byte-for-byte the build a student runs. Compliance is on your honour — and on your auditors — not on a kill switch we hold. That is a deliberate product decision, and it will not change.

06 · Procurement FAQ

The questions buyers actually ask

Does Goel° send any data anywhere?

No. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no usage tracking and no licence check-in. The only outbound connections the app makes are the transfers you start and the update check (Sparkle / GitHub Releases), which can be switched off in Settings. Downloads go directly from your machine to the server, peer or tracker you chose — nothing is relayed through us. This is verifiable in the source, and it is written into the commercial agreement as a warranty.

Where are credentials stored?

Site logins, SFTP passwords and portal credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain, never in plaintext config. The web-portal password is stored only as a salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 digest (210,000 iterations), so a leaked settings file does not disclose it. Full detail is in the security questionnaire.

Can we pay by invoice / purchase order?

Yes, for Business and Enterprise. Bank transfer, PO reference on the invoice, and the usual supplier-onboarding paperwork (W-9 or local equivalent, VAT ID, bank details on letterhead) are all supported. Net-30 is standard; longer terms are negotiable for Enterprise.

Is the licence perpetual or a subscription?

Business is a perpetual per-seat licence with 12 months of updates: when the update window ends, you keep running everything you have, you just stop receiving new versions until you renew. Enterprise is an annual site licence covering unlimited seats at the named entity for the term.

What counts as a seat?

One named user or one managed machine, whichever is easier for you to count. Rebuilding a laptop, or a user replacing their machine, does not consume a new seat. We do not audit seat counts technically — there is nothing in the software that could.

We are a non-profit / school. Do we really have to pay?

Almost certainly not. PolyForm Noncommercial names charitable organisations, educational institutions, public research organisations, public safety and health organisations, environmental protection organisations and government institutions as permitted uses outright — regardless of funding, paid staff or IT-managed machines. You do not need a licence from us and you do not need to ask. The exception is genuinely commercial activity run out of such an organisation, such as a spin-out company or a service sold to customers; reduced rates apply there and are granted routinely.

Can we evaluate before buying?

Yes, and there is no clock on it. Download the public build and run it. Evaluation inside a company is explicitly permitted for a reasonable assessment period; when you decide to keep it in production, that is when the licence is needed.

What about the third-party components?

Goel° bundles libtorrent-rasterbar, Boost, OpenSSL, libssh2, GRDB.swift and Sparkle, all under permissive licences (BSD-3-Clause, BSL-1.0, Apache-2.0, MIT). There is no copyleft component in the shipped binary. The full list with versions and licence texts is in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md and the SPDX-style SBOM.

Can it run air-gapped?

Yes. Disable the update check and the app makes no connection it was not told to make. The headless Linux daemon and the local web portal work entirely on your own network.

How are security issues handled?

Report privately to the security contact; you get an acknowledgement within one business day and a fix or mitigation plan within five. Business and Enterprise licensees are emailed directly when a security release ships; Enterprise licensees get pre-disclosure where the issue affects them. See support-sla.md.

What happens if you stop maintaining it?

The source is public and your licence is perpetual (Business) or runs to term (Enterprise), so nothing you have deployed stops working. Source-escrow arrangements can be written into an Enterprise agreement if your risk process requires one.
